Chapter 10 · The Birthday Issue

Happy 10th Birthday,
Jishaa 🎂

The final page of this chapter. Not the final page of our story.

Dear Jishaa,

Ten years ago, I became your Dad.

I didn't know exactly what that meant. I didn't know how many laughs we'd share, how many times you'd surprise me, or how quickly these ten years would pass.

But I do know one thing.

Being your Dad has been one of the greatest gifts of my life.

I've loved watching you learn. I've loved watching you grow. I've loved watching you become more and more yourself.

I hope you always stay curious. I hope you always stay kind. I hope you chase the things that make you happy.

And when life gets difficult, I hope you remember that you never have to face it alone.

Dad will always be cheering for you.
Today. Tomorrow. And every chapter that comes next.

Happy 10th Birthday, Jishaa!
Love, Dad ❤️
